## Post copy

The median home listed for sale in Greater Victoria right now is priced 7.4% above its government assessment.

We matched 15 live BC listings to their BC Assessment values and measured the gap directly, address by address. A few things stood out:

→ 80% of matched listings (12 of 15) are priced above assessment — only 3 are at or below it.
→ The gap isn't uniform across a few kilometres of the same metro area. Victoria listings skew +9.0% above assessment (n=9); Saanich listings skew -3.1% below it (n=4).
→ Range across the full sample: -6.4% to +35.7%. A single "market" median hides a lot of local variance.

None of this means a listing above assessment is overpriced, or one below it is a deal — assessed value lags the market by up to a year, and lot size, renovation status, and micro-location all move the fair gap. But it's a real starting point before you decide how far below asking to offer.
