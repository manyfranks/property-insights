# Qwoted pitch templates — Property Insights data notes

Reusable 3-sentence skeletons for responding to journalist source requests (HARO/Qwoted-style) covering real estate, housing affordability, rental yield, or property-tax/assessment stories. Fill the bracketed fields per request; keep to 3 sentences so it reads fast in a query inbox.

---

## Template 1 — BC Assessment-to-Asking-Price Gap

**Use for:** Canadian housing market stories, BC/Victoria real estate coverage, property assessment/tax stories, "is this listing overpriced" consumer pieces.

> I'm [Matt Francis / your title], founder of Property Insights, a real estate acquisition-intelligence tool that matches live listings against government assessment records to flag pricing gaps. In our most recent Greater Victoria sample, the median listing is priced 7.4% above its BC Assessment value — but that splits sharply by municipality (Victoria +9.0% median vs. Saanich -3.1% median), showing "the market" hides real block-by-block variance. Happy to pull a custom cut for [publication] — by city, price band, or property type — and can speak to the methodology (address-matched pairs, not extrapolated averages) and its limitations on the record.

Full data: https://www.propertyinsights.xyz/blog/bc-assessment-to-asking-price-gap

## Template 2 — US Rent-to-Price Ratio by County

**Use for:** US housing affordability stories, rental-investing/landlord coverage, "best places to buy a rental" roundups, appreciation-vs-cash-flow market comparisons.

> I'm [Matt Francis / your title], founder of Property Insights, and we ran the real-estate "1% rule" against Census data for 3,212 US counties to see which markets actually clear it. Only 29 do — nationally the median county sits at 0.467% monthly rent-to-price (5.6% gross yield), and the counties that clear 1% are uniformly low-cost rural markets (all under $125K median home value), while resort and coastal counties like Martha's Vineyard and Jackson Hole sit at the opposite extreme despite double-digit five-year appreciation. I can run a custom cut for [publication] — by state, metro vs. rural, or against a specific list of counties/cities you're covering — sourced from Census ACS and FHFA HPI with full margin-of-error notes.

Full data: https://www.propertyinsights.xyz/blog/us-rent-to-price-ratio-by-county

## Notes for reuse

- Swap `[Matt Francis / your title]` and `[publication]` per request; keep everything else as-is unless the request asks for a specific angle.
- Both templates lead with the citable topline stat, back it with one comparison finding, and close with a concrete offer of custom data — matches what editors scan for first in a pitch queue.
- If a reporter asks for a quote rather than data, pull directly from the "What This Means" section of the relevant blog post rather than improvising a new claim.
