Economics verdict — build the link-out flavor only

Verified Aug 8 research: no aggregator offers a self-serve embeddable quote form at our scale except SmartFinancial, whose pay-per-lead economics require completion volume that thin programmatic traffic can't deliver — a $1–5 finance-vertical CPC link approximates the revenue without the machinery. Hosting the form would also make us the PII collector (privacy-policy scope) and the TCPA consent-taker ($500–1,500 statutory damages per violation follow whoever collects consent). The embedded flavor shown above is therefore a future-state reference only. The link-out flavor keeps our pre-fill advantage — address/state/zip carried via URL params into the partner's hosted funnel (the Matic pattern) — with integration cost of "add query params to a link."

Mobile sticky bar

A sticky bar keeps the CTA in the thumb zone once the user scrolls past the in-flow one, and thumb-zone research on mobile tap targets is solid. The specific lift numbers usually quoted for sticky bars in this space aren't independently verified, though, so this one should ship as an A/B test, not a default.

V6

Comparison table (future state)

A list of three or more insurance quotes in a divide-y row is the core mechanic NerdWallet and Bankrate use for their comparison tables, and the comparison structure itself is what signals neutrality to the visitor — no single vendor looks favored. It needs three or more live vendors in the same vertical to avoid looking sparse or biased, so this is a future-state mock against pending applications.
