## Section 1 — Reusable answer blocks

### 50-word description
> Property Insights (propertyinsights.xyz) is a free real-estate acquisition-intelligence tool: enter a US or Canadian address and get an assessment-backed offer recommendation — valuation triangulation, market context, and risk signals. Covers all 50 US states (3,144 county pages) plus BC, Ontario, and Alberta. Built for buyers and investors evaluating a specific property.

### 150-word description
> Property Insights (propertyinsights.xyz) is a free web app that turns a single property address into an acquisition-intelligence report. Users — homebuyers and real-estate investors, skewed toward investors — enter a US or Canadian address and get an assessment-backed offer recommendation built from valuation triangulation (assessed value, comparable data, and modeled AVM where available), local market context, and risk signals (flood, over-assessment, momentum). Coverage spans all 50 US states, backed by 3,144 programmatic county market pages and 51 state indexes, plus BC, Ontario, and Alberta in Canada.
>
> The site is early-stage (launched 2026), growing through programmatic SEO and data-driven content (an assessment-gap calculator, methodology notes) rather than paid acquisition — no ad spend, no purchased email lists, no incentivized traffic. Every result page carries state-aware, mode-aware partner CTAs (insurance, lending, landlord tools) placed in context at the moment a user is evaluating a real property — a bottom-of-funnel, high-intent placement rather than a generic content link.

### "How will you promote us?"
> We don't run a content/blog site with outbound affiliate links — we run a property-analysis tool. Your program would be surfaced as a **contextual CTA on the result page itself**, immediately after a user has entered a specific property address and received our assessment-backed analysis. Placement is state-aware (only shown where you're relevant/licensed) and mode-aware (buyer vs. investor). Because the user has just supplied a real property and read a real analysis, the placement is bottom-of-funnel — closer to a comparison-shopping moment than a generic banner. Every CTA carries a "Sponsored" label and sits next to a plain-language FTC disclosure. We do not host lead-capture forms — it's a pure link-out, and no personal information about the user is passed to us or to you beyond what your own landing page collects.

### "Traffic / audience size"
> We're early-stage — the site launched in 2026 and traffic is still building through programmatic SEO (3,196 URLs in our sitemap, indexed via Google Search Console) rather than paid acquisition or purchased lists. We'd rather be honest about where we are than inflate a number: current volume is modest, but the trajectory is monthly URL/content growth (3,144 county market pages already live) and the traffic we do get is high-intent — visitors have entered a specific property address and are actively evaluating an acquisition, not casually browsing. We're applying now so the integration and disclosure work is done before volume arrives, and we're happy to reapply or share Search Console data once there's a meaningful history to show.

### Tax / payout block
- **Entity type**: Sole proprietor, individual (not a corporation)
- **Country**: Canada
- **Tax form**: W-8BEN (individual) — not W-8BEN-E — claiming Canada–US treaty benefits. Affiliate commissions are **business profits for services performed in Canada** (Article VII), not royalties — foreign-source, so line 10 is normally left blank. _(General info, not tax advice — accountant to bless.)_
- **W-8BEN line 10, when a network's form REQUIRES it** (reuse verbatim):
  - Article and paragraph: `VII(1)` · Rate: `0` · Type of income: `Business profits — affiliate/referral commissions for advertising services performed outside the United States; beneficial owner has no U.S. permanent establishment`
  - "Explain the additional conditions" field: `The beneficial owner is an individual resident of Canada within the meaning of Article IV of the Canada–U.S. income tax convention and is the beneficial owner of the income. The income consists of business profits — affiliate/referral commissions for advertising and referral services performed entirely outside the United States. Under Article VII(1), such profits are taxable only in Canada because the beneficial owner does not carry on business in the United States through a permanent establishment as defined in Article V: no office or fixed place of business in the United States, and no dependent agent acting there on the beneficial owner's behalf. As an individual resident of Canada, the beneficial owner is a qualifying person under the Limitation on Benefits article (Article XXIX-A). A 0% rate of withholding therefore applies.`
- **Payout preference**: PayPal preferred; wire transfer as fallback if PayPal is unsupported
- **Name/address/phone/email fields**: use placeholders `[FULL LEGAL NAME]`, `[FULL ADDRESS]`, `[PHONE]`, `[EMAIL]` — Matt fills in at submission time
- **Owner**: Matt Francis, Canadian sole proprietor

### Compliance credibility line (optional, use when a form has a free-text "tell us about your compliance practices" field)

The site publishes a privacy policy, terms of service, a US State Privacy Rights section, and a Do Not Sell/Share opt-out that honors the Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal.
